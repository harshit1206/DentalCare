OUR SERVICES

Comprehensive, Gentle Dental Care

Every treatment is planned conservatively and carried out using state-of-the-art painless technology for your complete comfort.

Single-Sitting Root Canal

Save infected teeth seamlessly. With computerized painless anesthesia and rotary instruments, most root canals are completed comfortably in just 45 minutes.

Starting from ₹4,500 (Depends on tooth location)

Clear Aligners & Braces

Straighten crooked teeth or close gaps discreetly without metal wires. Custom invisible aligners made with precision 3D digital scans for predictable, aesthetic results.

Starting from ₹45,000

Dental Implants

Permanent, natural-feeling replacement for missing teeth. Titanium implants topped with high-translucency zirconia crowns for lifelike chewing strength and aesthetics.

Starting from ₹25,000 (Per Implant)

Teeth Whitening & Cleaning

Gentle ultrasonic scaling to remove plaque and tartar, followed by safe laser whitening that lifts stubborn stains by several shades in a single relaxing session.

Starting from ₹1,500 (Cleaning & Polishing)

Zirconia Crowns & Veneers

Restore chipped, cracked, or discolored teeth with custom German CAD/CAM zirconia crowns and ceramic veneers, designed to blend naturally with your smile.

Starting from ₹6,000 (With warranty)

Family & Pediatric Care

Comprehensive preventive dental checkups, cavity fillings, and gentle pediatric treatments in a warm, welcoming environment that puts both kids and adults at ease.

OUR COMMITMENT

Why Patients Choose Aura Dental Studio

We built our practice on four principles to make every dental visit comfortable, transparent, and completely worry-free.

Gentle, comfort-focused dentistry

We use computerized local anesthesia and gentle rotary technology, ensuring treatments like fillings and root canals are virtually pain-free.

Appointments scheduled to minimise waiting

We value your busy schedule. Appointments are spaced appropriately and scheduled consultations start promptly without prolonged waiting.

High-standard sterilization

Strict European Class-B vacuum autoclave protocols. Sealed, sterile instrument pouches are opened right in front of you inside the operatory.

Honest, Clear Pricing

Transparent written treatment estimates upfront with zero hidden charges.
